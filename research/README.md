# Research guide

[Latest V8](../Version/V8/README.md) · [V0–V8 introductions](../Version/README.md) · [Complete results index](RESULTS.md) · [Implementation archive](../Version/IMPLEMENTATION.md) · [Project](../README.md)

V8 evaluation, analysis and publication are complete. Confirmation improvement did not transfer to full-benchmark table replacement. V8.4/V8.5 are reserved and unstarted; see the [version reservation](../Version/V8/results/large-lora-001/NEXT_VERSIONS.md). Historical status and proposals describe their original scope, not active experiments.

The research question is how to improve document parsing while preserving correct native content and measuring transfer beyond isolated regions. The objective includes an officially recognized OmniDocBench improvement and a generalizable research contribution; local scores alone do not establish either.
