## V3.1: original-image formula replacement

TeleOCR combines conservative PP-DocLayoutV3 layout proposals with PaddleOCR-VL1.6 formula recognition. Accepted geometry changes require fresh native recognition; ambiguous proposals retain the original result. The reported variant keeps the formula-replacement pipeline while bypassing PDF transcoding/resampling in favor of original-image input.

**Reported Overall: 97.6973.** The page count, baseline, failure handling and all seven component fields were not supplied. No paired gain can be established and no missing component is reconstructed from Overall.
